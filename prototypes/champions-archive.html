Every Champion, 1920–2025

Super Bowl winners, AFC and NFC champions, NFL champions before the merger, and the ten AFL champions, listed by season. Search a team to trace its history; open Title Count for franchise totals.

Seasons are labelled by the year the season started, so Super Bowl LX (played February 2026) is the 2025 season. Team names are shown as they were at the time; title counts group them by today's franchise. NFL titles from 1920 to 1932 were decided by league standings, not a title game (1932 was settled by a tiebreaker playoff). From 1966 to 1969 the NFL and AFL champions met in the Super Bowl; after the 1970 merger those slots became the NFC and AFC champions.
